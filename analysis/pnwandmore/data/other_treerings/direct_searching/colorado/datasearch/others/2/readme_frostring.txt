DESCRIPTION:
Earlywood and Latewood frost rings and light rings in bristlecone pines 
sampled on Almagre Mountain, Black Mountain, Mount Princeton, North Peak,
and Stanley Mountain from 569 B.C. to A.D. 1993, and comparison to 
reported frost rings at other bristlecone pine sites (data from Brunstein, 1995)

SITE NAMES: Almagre Mountain, Black Mountain, Mount Princeton, 
North Peak, Stanley Mountain
SITE CODES: AM, BM, MP, NP, SM
ELEVATION: 3353-3700 meters (AM), 3380-3540 (BM), 3585-3700 (MP), 
3540-3570 (NP), 3430-3550 (SM)
LATITUDE: 38 46 N (AM), 38 43 N (BM), 38 44 N (MP), 37 53 N (NP), 39 47 N (SM)
LONGITUDE: 104 59 W (AM), 105 41 W (BM), 106 13 W (MP), 105 2 W (NP), 
105 48 (SM)
SPECIES: Pinus Aristata,  species code PIAR
COMMON NAME: Rocky Mountain bristlecone pine
CHRONOLOGY TYPE: latewood frost ring, light ring, and earlywood frost ring
FIRST AND LAST YEAR: 569 B.C. to A.D. 1993 (AM), A.D. 152-1992 (BM), 
359 B.C. to A.D. 1992 (MP), A.D. 1536-1993 (NP), A.D. 1380-1993 (SM)
