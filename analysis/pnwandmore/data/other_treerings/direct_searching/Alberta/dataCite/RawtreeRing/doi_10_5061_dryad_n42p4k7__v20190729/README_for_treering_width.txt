In total tree-ring width of 164 white spruce trees from 33 provenances from 1983-2016. (missing values were show in NA)

the csv file includes 14 column: prov(provenance id), id(id of tree), year, trw(tree-ring width), detrend_raw(results after tradition detrending from whole samples),
avg(average width for each tree), std(standard deviation of the ring width for each tree), detrend_recover(based on detrend_raw value, also consider the average growth conditions from avg, and std to highlight growth differences among trees),
block (block of the common garden), Province(province of the provenances original from) with their coordinates (Latitude, Longitude, Elevation), new_region (33 provenances were classified into 6 larger regions for analysis in the study.
