D. C. Drake and Robert J. Naiman. 2007. Reconstruction of Pacific salmon abundance from riparian tree-ring growth. Ecological Applications 17:1523–1542.

Appendix E. Spectral analyses of reconstructed salmon abundance.

The spectra were calculated using a Tukey-Hamming window of five years. Any evenly spaced, uneven height peaks following the lowest frequency peak are interpreted as “shadows” of the dominant peak resulting from the Sin-Cos transforms used in production of the spectra (i.e., do not indicate true periodicities).
