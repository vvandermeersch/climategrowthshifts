Appendix D. Autocorrelation (ACF) and partial autocorrelation (PACF) analyses of differenced ring width (RWd) chronologies.

Autocorrelation (ACF) and partial autocorrelation (PACF) analyses of RWd chronologies. Autoregressive process is indicated by exponentially decreasing ACF and spikes in the first one or more PACF lag. Our detrending removes some AR process, but leaves the common AR process. Evidence of AR process is seen in the Drinkwater, Sashin and Kadashan and possibly the Disappearance RWd chronologies. The numbers of spikes in the PACF panel indicates the order of the autoregression (e.g., 1 in Drinkwater Ck). Moving average processes was not indicated (by spikes in the first few ACF lags and an exponentially declining PACF) at any of the sites.
